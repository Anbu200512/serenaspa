How sessions work

A cycle you can see and plan

Nothing hides in fine print. Your dashboard mirrors these four steps from the day you join.

  1. 01

    Sessions refresh monthly

    Every 30 days your plan refills its full session count automatically — no renewal forms, no lapses.

  2. 02

    Track it in the dashboard

    Sessions included, used and remaining for the live cycle are listed in your client dashboard, updated the moment you book.

  3. 03

    Watch the expiry date

    The exact cycle expiry sits beside your sessions, so you always know how many days are left to book.

  4. 04

    Upgrade any time

    Move to a bigger plan whenever you like — the new allowance applies immediately with prorated billing for the days remaining.
