How to choose

Not sure where to start?

Four quick signals point to the right ritual — and your therapist confirms the final choice during the consultation.

First visit? Swedish

Long, flowing strokes at light to medium pressure set a gentle baseline — the easiest way to meet our hands.

Tension from desk work? Deep tissue

Slow, firm pressure into the shoulders, neck and lower back where hours of sitting store their stress.

Mental reset? Aromatherapy

Essential oils blended by mood turn an hour on the table into quiet for a racing mind — lavender, eucalyptus or ylang-ylang.

Short on time? Head & Shoulder 45 min

Focused scalp, neck and shoulder work that fits a lunch break and still releases screen strain.
